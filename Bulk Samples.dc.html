What am I looking at?

Each point is one bulk RNA-seq profile from ARCHS4: its learned-identity sample summary token, reduced to two dimensions. Samples that the model represents similarly sit close together.

This map contains the exported exp8 samples, balanced across labels and studies; it is not the whole training corpus. Tissues and GEO study groups are annotations added after training. Nearby samples may share biology, technical effects, or both.
