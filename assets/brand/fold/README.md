# OmicsFM Fold identity

Selected design: an upright open F and a folded valley in the M.
The selected paths are stored in the generator and deskewed to remove their forward lean.
Wordmarks combine outlined lowercase Audiowide lettering with the selected mark at cap height.

Available in proteomics lime, bulk violet, single-cell pink, white and black.
Stacked variants put omics above FM, aligned by their visible outlines to equal widths.
Bulk FM uses #5100FD, matching the site's violet panels and hover colour.
All SVGs are font-independent. Use black variants on light backgrounds.
Regenerate with python scripts/build_fold_brand.py (fontTools and the bundled Audiowide font).
